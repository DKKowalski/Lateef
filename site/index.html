02 / A handle on access

It’s your browser. Still.

01

A session of its own

Each client sees the tabs it creates and those you explicitly share. A tab ID alone isn’t permission.

02

There’s an off switch

Disconnect in the extension. Revoke pairing in the CLI. Close a session and its agent-created tabs are cleaned up.

03

Local bridge. Shared content.

The bridge runs on your computer. Selected page content and files pass to your AI client and may reach its provider. That distinction matters.

The fine print, in normal type

A useful beta.
Not every browser task.

Requires Chrome, Node.js 22+ and an MCP client. Uploads are capped at 2 MiB total. Iframes, password entry, downloads and hardware-input-dependent sites aren’t supported. Gmail attachments still need live verification. macOS is tested; other platforms need validation. The companion is on npm; the extension is not yet on the Chrome Web Store.
