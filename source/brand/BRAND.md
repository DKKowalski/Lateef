# Lateef — brand identity

Chosen name: **Lateef**, pronounced **lah-TEEF**, from Arabic لطيف (gentle, pleasant, subtle). No domain, registry name or trademark reservation is implied.

Promise: **A little help, in the background.**

The mark is a clear L with a small independent tab, representing the user staying grounded while an agent works beside them. Use the single-colour version when a small accent cannot be reproduced reliably. The mark must remain legible at 16 px.

Palette: ink #1B1B1D, paper #F7F7F5, vermilion #FF4D21. Typography: locally served Geist under SIL OFL. No AI-provider or Google logo is part of the identity.

Assets: mark.svg (vector master); wordmark.svg; icon-512.png; wordmark.png (transparent export); extension/icons/ in 16, 32, 48 and 128 px. Source fonts and license are included in the website assets.

The extension/runtime preserves old CLI aliases and pairing state. Updating the brand does not change global MCP registrations or restart current clients.

Motion direction and implementation requirements are in MOTION_GUIDE.md.
